# The Entity

A vampiric presence lives behind the world of AGOSTO.

It is not a mascot and it does not dominate the project. It appears through the music and connects the fictional world with the dancefloor.

Its origin belongs somewhere between an ancient lineage, modern cities and sub-bass.

The face is pale. The eyes are marked with Xs. There are fangs, but the expression is ambiguous: somewhere between a traditional creature of horror and something strangely familiar.

It does not hunt for blood.

It looks for pulse.

Movement, sweat, resonance and the moment when a room stops behaving like a collection of individuals and begins moving as one body.

The entity becomes more tangible as tempo rises. At lower BPM it survives as fog, echoes and shadows. As the rhythm intensifies, it begins to cross into the physical world.

Its presence should never be completely explained.

Sometimes it speaks.

When it does, the language is short, physical and visual. Doors sweat. Floors remember. Canals breathe. The city bends under bass.

The entity does not arrive.

It becomes.
