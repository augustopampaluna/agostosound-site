AGOSTO is an Amsterdam-based DJ and electronic music producer working at the intersection of raw, industrial, dark and hypnotic techno.

Music has been part of his life from an early age. He started playing instruments at five and producing music at eleven, developing a background that moved through rock, metal and electronic music before techno became the central language of the project.

His current sound is built around dense atmospheres, repetitive tension, physical low-end and club-focused energy, usually moving between 135 and 145 BPM. As a DJ and performer, AGOSTO combines his own productions with carefully selected music while keeping a consistent sonic identity rather than relying on a fixed performance setup.

Beyond the dancefloor, AGOSTO is also a conceptual project. Releases, visuals and recurring symbols form fragments of a larger narrative universe influenced by nightlife, horror, cities and the relationship between rhythm and transformation.

Amsterdam is both the base of the project and an important part of its identity: its canals, architecture, fog, nightlife and nocturnal atmosphere repeatedly appear throughout the visual and narrative language surrounding the music.

The story never comes before the sound. It exists to extend it.
