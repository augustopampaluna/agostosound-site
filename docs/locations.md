# Locations

The world of AGOSTO uses Amsterdam as a base, but familiar places can shift when rhythm crosses the threshold.

## Prinsengracht Rift

A fracture running through the canal.

The water reacts to low frequencies and the opening becomes visible when the kick touches its surface.

The canal starts breathing.

## Jordaan Crypt

Narrow streets and crooked houses.

Architecture leans in every direction while the bass forces the body into alignment.

Crooked houses. Straight bass.

## A'DAM Nox

A high point above the city.

From here, the X can be projected across Amsterdam as a signal that the threshold is open.

## Vondelcrypt

An underground space beneath the park.

It cannot be found through maps or signs. The entrance appears through pulse.

## The Silent Floor

A space without rhythm.

Cold light. Static screens. Motionless bodies.

There is no resonance and nothing responds.

It represents the opposite of the world created by the music.
