# Manifesto

The music carries the story.

The artist does not explain the world.
He opens it.

The night is not dark.
The night is rhythmic.

I bite the BPM.

The door sweats.

The floor remembers.

The kick touches water.

The X opens slowly.

Amsterdam bends under bass.

The canal starts breathing.

No blood tonight.

Only bodies.
Only pulse.
Only heat.

The silence cannot follow us.

The light is too clean.

The room has teeth.

The crowd unlocks me.

Your skin keeps the tempo.

I do not arrive.

I become.
