01

World

AGOSTO is built around a narrative techno universe where each track acts as a fragment of something larger. A place, a memory, a tension or a transformation can appear for a few minutes and disappear again.

There is no single story to follow. The world reveals itself through sound, images and recurring signs.

The music carries the story. The artist only opens the door.

02

The Presence

At the center of the world is a vampiric presence — part traditional creature, part contemporary club spirit.

It does not exist to dominate the story or become a mascot. It appears around it: on walls, in reflections, inside artwork and between fragments of the music.

It does not look for blood. It is drawn to movement, sweat, resonance and collective energy.

When the city becomes quiet, it begins to appear.

03

The Mark

The X is the recurring sign of this world.

It can mean a crossing, a portal, an error or the moment when one reality begins to become another. Sometimes it appears clearly. Sometimes it is hidden inside a photograph, a visual or a release.

The crossed eyes are not decoration. They mean that the threshold has already been crossed.

Once you notice the X, you are already inside.

04

The Place

Amsterdam is the place where this presence can most often be found.

Canals, bridges, rain, bicycles, tilted houses, tunnels and reflected lights become part of the same nocturnal system. Familiar places can return under different names or behave differently once they enter the narrative.

A canal can become a rift. A street can become a passage. A club can become a refuge.

The city is not the setting. It is part of the instrument.

05

The Floor

The story is built in fragments. A release can reveal a place, a memory, a new sign or another piece of the presence without explaining the entire world.

Nothing needs to be understood beforehand. The lore exists around the music, not in front of it.

And the audience is not outside the story. The moment the music reaches the floor, bodies, movement and collective energy become part of it.

The crowd does not observe the world. It unlocks it.
